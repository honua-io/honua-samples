Public data and the agent task

Ask a stock MCP client: “Which unclosed exercise work orders share a road anchor with an exercise inspection? Cite order IDs, inspection IDs, asset IDs and due times.” Check each join in the record details.

Bridge records are a historical NBI snapshot, not a current safety assessment. Exercise road anchors do not identify bridges, inlets, culverts or signals. Inspections link to orders only by identical asset IDs.
