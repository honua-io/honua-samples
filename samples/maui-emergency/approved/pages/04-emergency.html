What to do in a tsunami warning

  1. Check the map. Orange is the evacuation zone. If you are in it, leave now.
  2. Go inland or uphill, on foot if you can. Stay out until the all-clear.
  3. Go to an open shelter if you have nowhere else to go. Bring medicine, water, ID and pet supplies.
  4. Avoid closed roads. Kahului Beach Road and Hobron Avenue are closed.
