Field Maps

Your existing field apps against Honua services

Field crews keep collecting with field apps that edit feature services or OGC API Features, including QField, with offline sync and conflict handling on Honua Server. Honua's own mobile SDK and Collect field app are in development and will follow this release.

Field-app compatibility, offline sync and conflict handling must be verified for the selected client, version and service configuration during the pilot.

Move in stages

A measured path to the next renewal

Illustrative planning windows, not delivery commitments. Keep the existing service available until its replacement passes acceptance.

  1. Weeks 1–2

    Stand up a pilot

    Choose a bounded dataset, an owner and acceptance checks.

  2. Weeks 2–4

    Publish alongside existing services

    Import a copy, retain source provenance and compare query results.

  3. Weeks 4–8

    Point clients at the pilot

    Verify authentication, rendering, queries and edits for each client.

  4. Months 2–4

    Rebuild the applications

    Validate the screens, accessibility and operational workflows with their users.

  5. At renewal

    Retire on your schedule

    Cut over only after acceptance, rollback and records-retention requirements are met.

Prove the fit

Check the clients your team uses

This is a pilot checklist, not a certification or compatibility guarantee.

  • ArcGIS Pro: required FeatureServer layer operations.
  • ArcGIS JavaScript applications: MapServer rendering, queries and authentication.
  • Parcel lookup: identifier, address and spatial queries.
  • Emergency operations: required WMS and FeatureServer behavior.
  • Field Maps / QField: edits, offline sync, conflicts and reconnect recovery.
  • QGIS: required OGC API Features operations.

Before you switch

Is Honua certified by Esri?

No. Test the published GeoServices REST and OGC interfaces against each client's needs. Product names describe compatibility contexts and do not imply endorsement.

Can we run it ourselves?

Honua Server is source available under the FairCore license. Review the published license and your deployment requirements before adopting it.
