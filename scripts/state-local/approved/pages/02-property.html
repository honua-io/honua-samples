Demo answer
Planning review required. This scripted example shows the layers to check. Confirm permitted uses, coastal requirements and flood standards with Planning.
  • State land use: Urban. The state leaves use decisions to county zoning here.
  • Zoning: B-2 Community business is the mapped zoning designation; confirm permitted uses with Planning. The community plan source is pending. Check the Maui Island Plan growth boundary separately.
  • Special Management Area. The parcel is inside the coastal SMA, so Planning must assess the project before a building permit: exemption, minor permit or major permit.
  • Flood zone AE, base flood elevation must be verified. Ask the County floodplain administrator to verify applicable development and substantial-improvement requirements.
  • Tsunami evacuation zone. School exercise anchors are not activated shelters. Review the 3.2 ft sea-level-rise exposure overlay.
